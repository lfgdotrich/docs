---
title: Borrowing
description: Borrow BNB by locking LFG.RICH tokens as collateral.
---

LFG.RICH includes collateral borrowing for official tokens.

Users can lock tokens as collateral and borrow BNB against the token's floor value. Borrowing is based on the token's protocol floor, not temporary market spikes.

## Key properties

- Borrowing power is based on floor value.
- Locked collateral is removed from circulating supply while the borrow position is active.
- Borrowing has no liquidation mechanism.
- Borrowing has no ongoing interest.
- A fixed one-time **3% borrow fee** is deducted upfront.
- The 3% fee is used to support and increase the token's floor.
- Partial repayment unlocks a proportional amount of collateral.
- Full repayment unlocks the remaining collateral.

## Borrowing flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose collateral">
    The user chooses how many tokens to lock.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the hook">
    The user approves the Uniswap V4 Hook to transfer the selected token amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Borrow">
    The user calls `borrow(poolId, amount)`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collateral is locked">
    The hook transfers the selected tokens into collateral accounting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="BNB is sent to the borrower">
    The borrower receives the net BNB amount after the fixed 3% fee.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Repayment flow

Users repay by sending BNB to:

```solidity
repay(bytes32 poolId)
```

If the repayment is partial, the protocol unlocks a proportional amount of collateral.

If the repayment closes the debt, the remaining collateral is unlocked.

## Borrow more

If the floor rises after a borrow position is opened, the user's existing collateral may support additional borrowing.

Developers can estimate this with:

```solidity
estimateBorrowMore(bytes32 poolId, address user)
```

and then call:

```solidity
borrowMore(bytes32 poolId)
```

when additional value is available.
