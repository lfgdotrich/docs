title: Create a Token
description: Launch an official LFG.RICH token.
---

LFG.RICH lets creators launch official tokens directly from the app.

Each official token is created through the LFG.RICH factory and connected to the platform's bonding-curve, floor, fee, and borrowing mechanics.

## What you need

Before creating a token, prepare:

- Token name.
- Token symbol.
- Token logo.
- Short description.
- Website, X/Twitter, Telegram, or other community links.
- Trading fee selection between **1% and 10%**.
- BNB for gas.

## Creation flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect wallet">
    Connect a wallet on BNB Smart Chain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter token details">
    Add the token name, symbol, logo, description, and links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose trading fee">
    Select the permanent trading fee for the token. This fee applies to buys and sells.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create token">
    Submit the creation transaction from your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Token becomes official">
    After confirmation, the token appears as an official LFG.RICH token and can be traded through the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Trading fee

The creator-selected trading fee is permanent.

The total fee is split between:

- A fixed **0.3%** LFG.RICH platform fee.
- The remaining portion, which goes to the token's floor boost.

Example: with a **5%** total trading fee, **0.3%** is the platform fee and **4.7%** goes to floor boost.

## Token metadata

Token metadata such as logo, description, and social links is displayed across the app and can be used by integrations such as token pages, trackers, dashboards, and buybots.
