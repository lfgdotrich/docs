---
title: Borrow and Repay
description: Lock LFG.RICH tokens as collateral and borrow BNB.
---

LFG.RICH supports borrowing against official token collateral.

Borrowing uses the token's floor value, so the collateral value is based on protocol floor mechanics instead of short-term market price.

## Borrow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a token page">
    Choose an official LFG.RICH token that you hold.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose collateral">
    Select how many tokens to lock as collateral.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review borrow amount">
    Review the estimated BNB amount, borrow fee, and locked collateral.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve token if needed">
    Approve the contract to lock your selected token amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm borrow">
    Confirm the borrow transaction in your wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Borrow fee

Borrowing has a fixed one-time fee of **3%**.

The fee is deducted upfront. For example, if the gross borrow value is `1 BNB`, the user receives `0.97 BNB` after the `0.03 BNB` fee.

## Borrow more

If the token floor rises after borrowing, the same locked collateral can support more borrow value. Users can use **Borrow More** to borrow the additional available BNB.

## Repay

Users repay BNB to unlock collateral.

- Partial repayment unlocks a proportional amount of collateral.
- Full repayment unlocks the remaining locked collateral.

## Liquidations and interest

LFG.RICH borrowing has no liquidation mechanism and no ongoing interest. Collateral remains locked until repayment.
