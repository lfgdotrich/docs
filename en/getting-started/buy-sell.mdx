---

Official LFG.RICH tokens can be bought and sold through the LFG.RICH app.

The trading flow uses BNB on BNB Smart Chain and applies each token's bonding-curve, fee, reserve, and floor logic.

## Buying

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a token page">
    Choose the official LFG.RICH token you want to buy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter BNB amount">
    Enter how much BNB you want to spend.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review estimate">
    Review the estimated token amount, price impact, and fees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm transaction">
    Confirm the buy transaction in your wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Selling

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a token page">
    Choose the official LFG.RICH token you want to sell.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter token amount">
    Enter how many tokens you want to sell.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review estimate">
    Review the estimated BNB output, price impact, and fees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve if needed">
    If the token allowance is not enough, approve the token first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm transaction">
    Confirm the sell transaction in your wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fees

Each token has a creator-selected trading fee between **1% and 10%**.

The fee is split into:

- **0.3%** platform fee.
- The remaining percentage for floor boost.

## Slippage

Slippage protects users from receiving much less than expected when market conditions change between estimation and transaction confirmation.

Use conservative slippage settings when trading volatile tokens.
